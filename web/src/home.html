Driverless Yishun

Could driverless shuttles carry every bus ride inside Yishun?

On a school-term weekday, — bus rides start and end inside the town. This study replays every one through a fleet of on-demand shuttles and compares it, ride by ride, with today's buses, on LTA's own fare-card and live bus data. In the model, — ten-seat shuttles keep up with the bus in every hour.
